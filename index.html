About VEX Robot Corp

Driving Innovation in Africa

VEX Robot Corp is dedicated to bringing cutting-edge robotics technology to the African continent. We partner with Startup Africa to empower the next generation of innovators, engineers, and problem-solvers.

Our mission is to make robotics education and competition accessible to schools, universities, and innovation hubs across Africa, fostering STEM skills and preparing youth for the future economy.
